# Ross Lake / Desolation Peak — Trip Planning Document
## What This Is
A trip planning HTML document for a 6–7 day kayak + hiking expedition on
Ross Lake in North Cascades National Park, July 2026. Built for Wellesley
and Phil, two experienced hikers who have summited Desolation Peak ~12 times
on foot but never approached by boat.
## The Trip
- Launch from Ross Lake Resort (rental kayaks)
- Paddle north, targeting island camps (Ten Mile Island, Cat Island) as
  primary campsites with named alternates
- Stash kayaks at Desolation dock (14.1 mi from resort)
- Hike up and spend at least 1 night at Desolation Camp (separate NPS permit)
- Visit Jim Henterly, the lookout caretaker, at the summit (6,102 ft)
- Paddle back south over remaining days
- Permits via April 15 lottery window on recreation.gov
## Three Itinerary Options
- Option A: Classic 6-day route (1 summit night)
- Option B: 7-day with 2 nights at Desolation Camp ("The Jim Version")
- Option C: 7-day adding Devil's Dome side trip from Devil's Junction
## Key Facts
- Desolation dock: 14.1 mi from resort (kayak stash point, trailhead)
- Cat Island: 13 mi (1 mi south of dock, island camp, 4 sites)
- Ten Mile Island: 10 mi (island camp, 3 sites, primary northbound staging)
- Devil's Junction: 9 mi (Devil's Dome TH, 1 site — book immediately April 15)
- Lightning Creek Boat Camp: 12 mi (5 sites, near dock, good fallback)
- Desolation Camp: separate NPS permit, 1 group max, no fires, no water
- All lake camps require NPS backcountry permit (recreation.gov)
- Afternoon winds and whitecaps are a serious hazard — always paddle before noon
